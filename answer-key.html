Blocked its attack with their shield and struck it with their sword

The soldiers hid in fear. Guru Ji blocked the lion's attack with their shield and quickly struck it with their sword.

500 coins first, and 5,000 more once the task was done

Chandu paid 500 coins first and promised another 5,000 coins when the task was done.

6

What lie did Chandu order the astrologer to tell the emperor?

The lie was that Emperor Jahangir had been cursed and would die if he did not act fast.

Do all of this in Gwalior prison for 40 days

Bathe three times a day; meditate continuously; and do it all in Gwalior prison for 40 days.

8

Who did the lie say could complete the tasks for the emperor?

Only someone called “Hargobind”

Only someone called "Hargobind" could complete the tasks, and Chandu then suggested that only Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji fitted the description.

9

False

False. Guru Ji knew the emperor had been lied to, but agreed anyway: they knew they had to enter Gwalior prison for a different reason.

10

Who did Guru Ji find in Gwalior prison?

Many kings who had been wrongly imprisoned by the emperor

Many of the prisoners were kings who had been wrongly imprisoned by the emperor. They were covered in lice, in dirty, ripped clothes, and many could hardly walk.

4:07
11

A beautiful robe covered in a strong poison

Chandu had a beautiful robe made and covered it in a strong poison, hoping it would kill the Guru once worn.

4:48
12

Who stopped the poisoned robe from reaching Guru Ji?

Bhai Hardas Ji, the prison warden

Bhai Hardas Ji, the prison warden, had already heard of Chandu's plan and stopped the robe being delivered.

Simran = meditation, Seva = selfless service

Guru Ji started a routine of meditation and selfless service, known as 'Simran' and 'Seva'.

prisoner

The emperor had forgotten about Guru Ji, so the Sikhs realised they had to remind him themselves.

Bhai Jetha Ji and Bhai Pirana Ji

Bhai Jetha Ji and Bhai Pirana Ji snuck past the guards and found the emperor asleep.

That it was proof the curse was real, so Guru Ji should stay locked in prison

Chandu said this proved the curse was real, and advised keeping Guru Ji locked in prison.

18

After visiting as lions a second time, what did Bhai Jetha Ji disguise himself as?

A doctor

The terrified emperor called for a doctor, and Bhai Jetha Ji disguised himself as one.

19

That all the falsely imprisoned kings would have to be released too

The kings did not want to be without the Guru. Guru Ji said that if the Guru was released, all the falsely imprisoned kings would have to be released too.

Only those who could hold onto the Guru's robe as the Guru left would also be free to go.
